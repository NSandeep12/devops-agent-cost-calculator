Pricing basis & assumptions

Rate. All DevOps Agent activity is billed at $0.0083 per agent-second — Investigations (Incident Response), Evaluations (Site Reliability), and On-demand SRE tasks (Chat & Custom SRE Agents) share the one rate. Agent-time is cumulative active working time per task; idle/waiting time is not billed.
Source: AWS DevOps Agent Pricing.

Support credit offset. Customers on paid Support plans receive monthly DevOps Agent credits = plan % × prior-month AWS Support charge: 100% Unified Operations, 75% Enterprise, 30% Business+. Credits apply toward usage at $0.0083/agent-second, are issued by the 10th of the month, and expire month-end if unused (the "credit wasted" figure). AWS Partner-Led Support (PLS) / MSP customers receive credits through the partner process at a custom discount % that differs from Enterprise/UOps and is not public pricing — select Custom (PLS / MSP) in the plan dropdown and enter your own % to estimate. (These PLS percentages are intentionally not hardcoded, as they may not be official.)
Source: AWS DevOps Agent Pricing (Credits for AWS Support Customers).

Free trial (not modelled above). New DevOps Agent customers get a 2-month trial; each trial month includes up to 10 agent spaces, 20 hours of investigations, 15 hours of evaluations, and 20 hours of on-demand SRE tasks. Pay-as-you-go applies beyond those limits / after the trial.

Category mapping. "Code review" is billed under Evaluations (validating deployment of code changes / incident prevention). "Custom-agent runs" and "Chats" are billed under On-demand SRE tasks. This calculator keeps them as separate planning rows for clarity; all bill at the same rate.

Default durations come from the worked examples on the pricing page: investigations ~8 min, evaluations ~15 min, chats ~0.5 min, custom-agent runs ~2 min. Code review defaults to 5 min (editable — no official example). Because code-review run-time is right-skewed — a few large diffs run several times longer than the median — the large-diff tail control blends a typical and a large-diff duration (effective = (1−large%)×typical + large%×large-diff) so a single average doesn't understate cost at real PR volume. Excluded: connected-service charges (CloudWatch Logs Insights queries, trace retrievals) are billed separately at standard rates and are not in these figures.

Estimates based on public AWS pricing as of September 2026.
